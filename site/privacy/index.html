Overview

Opencoode is an open-source mobile client for connecting to self-hosted OpenCode and Pi servers that you run and control. The developer of Opencoode does not operate any backend servers for the app and does not collect, store, track, or sell your personal data.

Data We Do Not Collect

Opencoode does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting services, or remote CDNs. The developer receives no information about your identity, device, server addresses, credentials, prompts, files, or usage.

Permissions and Device Features

Opencoode requests access to device features only when you actively use them:

  • Camera (CAMERA): Used solely to scan pairing QR codes generated by your OpenCode or Pi server. Camera frames are processed locally on your device in real time and are never saved, recorded, or transmitted. Opencoode does not request or use microphone permissions.
  • Files and Media (Document Picker): Used only when you explicitly choose files or images to attach to a prompt or import a session. Selected files are read locally and sent only to the server you connected to.
  • Clipboard: Accessed only when you explicitly paste a pairing link or copy content within the app.
  • Network Access (INTERNET): Used exclusively to communicate with the OpenCode or Pi server URLs you configure or scan, and to open external documentation links when you tap them.

Local Data Storage

All app state stays on your device:

  • Secure Storage: Saved server URLs, usernames, passwords/access tokens, last active workspace identifiers, and app preferences (such as theme and haptics settings) are stored locally using the operating system’s secure storage (expo-secure-store).
  • Local Cache: Unsent message drafts, model preferences, and temporary files used when attaching, importing, exporting, or sharing files are stored in the app’s local cache directory.

User-Configured Servers and Third-Party Services

When you connect Opencoode to an OpenCode or Pi server, your prompts, attachments, terminal input, and session data are sent directly between your device and the server address you specified (over HTTPS or HTTP, depending on the URL you configure).

You are responsible for the security and privacy of the servers you connect to. Any AI model providers, MCP servers, or plugins configured on your server process data under their own privacy policies, not this policy.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because the developer stores no user data, all data is controlled locally on your device:

  • You can remove individual servers and their credentials at any time inside the app under Settings → Servers → Manage.
  • You can erase all locally stored credentials, preferences, and cached drafts by clearing the app’s storage in your device settings or uninstalling Opencoode.

Children’s Privacy

Opencoode is a developer tool not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version and effective date will be posted at this URL and in the Opencoode source repository.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact SphericalKat at opencoode@kat.bio or open an issue at https://github.com/SphericalKat/opencoode/issues.
